## Tiers

### Mod features:

* **`Auto-detect kit:`** Press **Y** (editable) to auto-detect the gamemode you are playing, allowing you to easily switch and see opponent tiers in that specific mode.
* **`See nearest player stats:`** Press **H** (editable) to open the closest player's search result screen.
* **`Search screen:`** See all tiers, peaks, regions, points, global rankings, and skin with **/tiers name**.
* **`Change gamemodes and their position:`** Choose a gamemode for each ranking site and decide whether to display it on the left or right.
* **`Cycle keybinds:`** Press **U** and **I** (editable) to easily cycle between the right and left displayed gamemodes.
* **`Change preset styles:`** Change icon and color presets within the config screen.
* **`Display region:`** View which region players are in.
* **`Display gamemodes:`** Choose whether to display the selected mode, adaptive highest, or absolute highest tier.
* **`Chat and Tablist support:`** In addition to nametags, tiers can be displayed both on the tablist and in the chat.

### Screen config (ModMenu or /tiers -config):

* **`Toggle Tiers:`** Toggles the mod on / off.
* **`Toggle Region:`** Toggles the region display next to the tier.
* **`Toggle Icons:`** Toggles the icons next to the tier.
* **`Toggle Peak Tiers:`** Toggles peak tiers. For example, if you had HT3 and were demoted to LT3, you'll see **^HT3**.
* **`Toggle Tab:`** Toggles Tiers on the tablist.
* **`Toggle Chat:`** Toggles Tiers in chat.
* **`Toggle Dynamic Separator:`** Decides if the separator (**|**) matches the tier color or remains gray.
* **`Displayed Tier:`** Decides whether to display the selected mode, adaptive highest, or absolute highest.
* **`Auto kit detect:`** Toggles Tiers displaying the gamemode based on your current inventory.
* **`Left and Right gamemode switcher:`** Switches between gamemodes of the selected provider.
* **`Position Settings:`** Decides if the specific provider's gamemodes are displayed on the right, left, or neither.
* **`Icon and color presets:`** Switch between classic, PvPTiers, and MCTiers styled icons.
* **`Preview Switcher:`** Decides whether to preview the default profile (ItzRealMe) or your own character.
* **`Clear cache:`** Clears the player cache (/tiers -clear).
* **`Reset settings:`** Resets the settings to their defaults (/tiers -reset).

### Other:

> Want to manually modify the icons or tier colors? Edit the [default texture pack](https://github.com/PvPTiers/Tiers/tree/master/src/main/resources/resourcepacks) and add it to your resource pack folder.

> Settings will be saved in your config folder and updated at runtime.

> The mod is client-side (works on multiplayer servers).
